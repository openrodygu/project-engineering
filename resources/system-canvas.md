# System canvas

One page for designing the project engineering system for a team or a project. Fill it in by hand or copy it into a note. Keep it short enough to review in five minutes.

| Workflow | [How work moves] | [Brief, board, commit] | [Name] | [Daily] |
| Communication | [How status travels] | [Update, decision] | [Name] | [Weekly] |
| Knowledge | [How context survives] | [Record, note] | [Name] | [As needed] |
| Planning and tracking | [How work is sized and measured] | [Plan, metric] | [Name] | [Weekly] |

## 3. Workflow

- Where does work enter?
- What are the statuses?
- What counts as done?
- What happens when work is blocked?
- Where is the shared view of record?

## 4. Communication

- Who needs a status update, and how often?
- Where do decisions get recorded?
- How does a blocker reach the person who can remove it?
- What is the one channel for urgent coordination?

## 5. Knowledge

- Where does the project brief live?
- Where do decision records live?
- What must survive a person leaving?
- What is private, and what is shared?

## 6. Planning and tracking

- How large is a task?
- How many tasks are in progress at once?
- Which milestones matter?
- Which three metrics will inform a decision?

## 7. Cadence

| When | What | Who | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | [Update own work] | [Everyone] | [Current task status] |
| Weekly | [Review the board] | [Owner] | [Next week list] |
| Per milestone | [Check scope and risk] | [Team] | [Decision or reset] |
| Per project | [Close and reflect] | [Team] | [Retrospective note] |
