# Weekly review checklist

Ten minutes, once a week. The goal is a current view and a clear next week.

## The board

- [ ] Every active task has an owner.
- [ ] Every active task has a status.
- [ ] Blocked tasks say what they are waiting for.
- [ ] Finished tasks are marked done or cancelled.
- [ ] Finished work is archived.
- [ ] Due dates are real, not invented.

## Flow

- [ ] Count tasks in progress. Is the number small enough?
- [ ] Find overdue work and decide: reschedule, split, or drop.
- [ ] Find work that has not moved in two weeks.
- [ ] Check the next milestone and its date.
- [ ] Check dependencies that cross a team or a project.

## Decisions and risks

- [ ] Record any decision made this week.
- [ ] Name the top risk and what would reduce it.
- [ ] Note anything the team needs to know.

## Next week

- [ ] Pick the few outcomes that matter most.
- [ ] Give each one an owner.
- [ ] Remove or park work that does not fit.
- [ ] Share a short status update if the team needs one.

## Done when

- [ ] The board matches reality.
- [ ] The next week has a short, owned list.
- [ ] One decision or risk is written down.
