# Skill template

A skill page teaches one practice and produces one artifact. Start with a quick start, add chapters only when they earn their place.

## The rule

> Quick start first, chapters later.

A reader should be able to stop after **Do this now** and still have something useful.

## Shape of a skill page

1. **Title and one-line promise** — what the reader will be able to do.
2. **Do this now** — a 15–30 minute exercise on a real project.
3. **What it is** — the minimum mental model.
4. **Go deeper** — optional chapters.
5. **Where this fits** — links to the workspace and templates.
6. **Common mistakes** — what to avoid.
7. **Next skill** — a suggestion, not a requirement.

## Copy this skeleton

```markdown
# [Skill name]

[One sentence: what this skill helps you do.]

## Do this now

Time: [15–30] minutes.

1. [Concrete step]
2. [Concrete step]
3. [Concrete step]

You will produce: [artifact].

Stop here if that is enough.

## What it is

[The minimum model. Three to five short paragraphs or a small table.]

## Go deeper

1. [Chapter — the next useful question]
2. [Chapter — a common edge case]

## Where this fits

- [Related skill](../guides/README.md)
- [Template or workspace](../templates/README.md)

## Common mistakes

- [Mistake and the fix]
- [Mistake and the fix]

## Next skill

[Suggested follow-up](../guides/README.md). Not required.
```

## Write it well

- **Name the artifact.** "A brief" beats "an understanding."
- **Timebox the quick start.** Put the minutes in the heading.
- **Use a real project.** Hypothetical examples are harder to finish.
- **Keep chapters independent.** A reader should be able to jump to one.
- **Use plain words.** Say "use," not "utilize." Say "start," not "initiate."
- **Link out, do not duplicate.** Point to the handbook or a template.
- **Cut anything that produces no artifact or decision.**

## Test before you publish

- [ ] Can a reader finish **Do this now** in one sitting?
- [ ] Does it produce a file, note, or decision?
- [ ] Does every link resolve?
- [ ] Can the reader stop after the quick start?
- [ ] Did you avoid turning the page into a rulebook?
