# Reading list

Short and curated. Read one source, apply one artifact, then decide whether to keep going.

## Project management

- **Making Things Happen** — Scott Berkun. Practical project leadership without ceremony.
- **Shape Up** — Ryan Singer. Free online at <https://basecamp.com/shapeup>. Useful for scope, appetite, and betting on work.
- **The Mythical Man-Month** — Frederick Brooks. Still the clearest account of why adding people late rarely helps.

## Git and version control

- **Pro Git** — Scott Chacon and Ben Straub. Free at <https://git-scm.com/book>. Read the branching and remote chapters first.
- **Git reference** — <https://git-scm.com/docs>. Use it for commands, not for learning the model.
- [Git workflows](../guides/git/06-workflows.md) — the branching and merge approach used in this repo.

## Obsidian and tools

- **Obsidian help** — <https://help.obsidian.md>. Start with links, properties, and vaults.
- **dotpm docs** — <https://dotpm.pm/docs/install>. For the shared team vault.
- **TaskNotes docs** — <https://tasknotes.dev/obsidian/>. For the personal execution vault.
- [Obsidian guide](../guides/obsidian/) — how the two vaults fit together in this repo.

## Team lead and communication

- **The Manager's Path** — Camille Fournier. A grounded guide to leading people at each level.
- **High Output Management** — Andy Grove. Classic on meetings, decisions, and leverage.
- **Radical Candor** — Kim Scott. Useful for feedback that is both clear and kind.
- [Team lead guide](../guides/team-lead/) — the practices used in this repo.

## How to use this list

1. Pick one source that matches your current problem.
2. Write down one idea you will try.
3. Apply it on a real project.
4. Keep it only if it reduces friction.
