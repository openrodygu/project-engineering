# Adopt in a day

You do not need a quarter to improve your system. One focused day is enough to adopt one practice.

## Morning: pick one skill

Choose one skill from the [catalog](../guides/README.md).

Read only:

- The skill README.
- Its **Do this now** section.
- The first chapter if you need context.

Do not read the whole skill.

## Midday: produce the artifact

Do the quick start on a real project, not a hypothetical one.

Examples:

- Write the project brief.
- Create the local Git repo and make a commit.
- Copy the two vaults and open them.
- Create tomorrow’s top three tasks.
- Write the one-on-one agenda.

The artifact is the point. If you finish with only notes, you have not adopted anything yet.

## Afternoon: use it once

Use the artifact in a real conversation or work session.

- Share the brief with one person.
- Push the commit to the practice remote.
- Open the project in the team vault.
- Use the personal task list for one day.
- Run the one-on-one with the agenda.

Notice what feels awkward. That is useful data.

## End of day: decide

Answer three questions:

1. Did this reduce friction?
2. What was annoying or unclear?
3. Will I keep it, change it, or drop it?

Keep only what earned its place.

## The one-day rule

> Adopt one practice per day. Do not adopt a whole system at once.

A small change you keep beats a large change you abandon.

## Try it

Schedule a one-day adoption block.

Write down:

- The skill.
- The artifact.
- The real project.
- The person you will share it with.
- The question you will ask at the end of the day.
