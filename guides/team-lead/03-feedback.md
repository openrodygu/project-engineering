# Feedback

Feedback is information that helps someone adjust. It is not a verdict on their character.

## Make it specific

Use three parts:

1. **Behavior** — what you observed.
2. **Impact** — what it caused.
3. **Request** — what to do next time.

Example:

> In yesterday's review you approved the change before reading the migration section. The team merged a risky step without a check. Next time, please read the migration and ask one question before approving.

That is easier to act on than "you need to be more careful".

## Timing

Give feedback close to the event. A comment saved for a monthly meeting loses the detail and feels like an ambush.

- **Praise:** as soon as you notice, and in public when it helps.
- **Correction:** soon, and in private unless it affects the whole team.

## Ask before you tell

Sometimes the person already knows. Start with a question:

- "How did that review feel to you?"
- "What would you do differently next time?"

If they see the issue, you can skip the lecture. If they do not, be direct.

## Separate observation from judgment

| Judgment | Observation |
| --- | --- |
| "You are not committed." | "You missed the last two checkpoints without telling me." |
| "You are difficult." | "You pushed back on the plan after the decision was made." |
| "Great work." | "Your test caught the null case before release. That saved us a hotfix." |

Specific praise matters as much as specific correction. It tells people what to repeat.

## Receiving feedback

You will get feedback too. Listen without defending. Ask for an example. Say what you will do. Then do it. A lead who cannot take feedback will not get honest feedback for long.

## When it is serious

If a problem repeats, say so plainly and connect it to expectations. Write down what was agreed and when you will check again. Do not let a serious issue sit in hints for months.

## Try it

Write one piece of feedback you have been avoiding, using behavior, impact, and request. Then write one piece of specific praise for the same person. Deliver both within a week.
