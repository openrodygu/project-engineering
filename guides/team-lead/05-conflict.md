# Conflict

Conflict is normal. Avoided conflict is what gets expensive.

Most team conflict comes from three sources: unclear expectations, competing priorities, or a person feeling unheard. Address the source, not just the symptom.

## Address it early

A small issue handled in a private conversation is a correction. The same issue after three months of resentment is a crisis.

Signs to act on:

- A tone change in messages.
- Someone withdrawing from discussions.
- Repeated complaints about the same person.
- Work slowing because two people will not coordinate.

## A simple conversation structure

1. **Facts** — what happened, without interpretation.
2. **Impact** — what it caused for the work or the team.
3. **Interests** — what each person needs.
4. **Next step** — one concrete agreement.

Example:

> "The last two handoffs arrived after the deadline. That put the release check at risk. I need the handoff by Thursday so the check has a day. What do you need to make that possible?"

That names the problem and invites a solution. It does not assign motives.

## Separate the people from the problem

- Talk about the behavior, not the person.
- Assume good intent until facts say otherwise.
- Do not collect allies before you have talked to the person.
- Keep the conversation private unless the team needs to know.

## When you are part of the conflict

Say so. "I was part of this too" lowers the temperature. Ask what you missed. Be willing to change your position if the facts support it.

## When it does not resolve

If a conflict repeats or affects the whole team:

1. Restate the shared goal.
2. Name the disagreement plainly.
3. Set a decision mode and a date.
4. Write the agreement down.
5. Follow up.

If behavior is harmful or repeated, move from coaching to a formal process. Do not handle harassment or safety issues as ordinary team friction.

## Try it

Write a short script for a conflict you are avoiding. Use facts, impact, interests, and one request. Practice it once out loud. Then book a private conversation within the week.
