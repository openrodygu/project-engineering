# Role and mindset

Moving from doing the work to leading the work is a change in what you are measured on. Your output is now the team's output, not your personal task list.

## Three jobs

**Direction.** People need to know what matters, what can wait, and why. A team that does not know the goal will optimize for the wrong thing.

**People.** Each person needs clear expectations, honest feedback, and a path to grow. That takes regular conversation, not an annual form.

**System.** Work should move, blockers should surface, and decisions should stick. Your job is to improve the system, not to personally push every item through it.

## What changes

| From | To |
| --- | --- |
| I finish tasks | I make it possible for others to finish |
| I make every decision | I set which decisions are mine, shared, or theirs |
| I am the best coder | I help the team get better |
| I know the status | The team can see the status |
| I avoid conflict | I address it early and directly |

You will still do technical work in many teams. The difference is priority: when the team needs direction or unblocking, that comes first.

## The trap of taking over

Taking over feels efficient. It is usually a loan against future capacity. The other person does not learn, you become the bottleneck, and the next similar task comes back to you.

When work is stuck, ask:

1. Is the goal clear?
2. Does the person have the skill and the authority?
3. Is something outside their control blocking them?

Fix that, then step back.

## Be predictable

People work better when they know how you operate:

- When you are available.
- How decisions get made.
- What "good" looks like.
- What happens when something goes wrong.

Predictability is not softness. It is how a team stops guessing.

## Try it

Write two lists:

- **Only I can do this** — decisions, context, or relationships that genuinely need you.
- **Someone else could own this** — work you are holding that another person could do with context and support.

Pick one item from the second list. That is your next delegation.
