# Time blocking

A task list says what to do. A calendar says when. Time blocking puts the two together so the day has a shape instead of hoping focus appears.

## Block the work, not the wish

A block is a real appointment with yourself. Give it a start, an end, and one outcome.

Bad block: "Work on project."
Good block: "Draft the vendor comparison intro, 09:00–10:00."

The second one tells you when you are done.

## A simple day shape

```text
08:30  Plan the day (15 min)
09:00  Deep work: hardest task (90 min)
10:30  Break
10:45  Deep work: second task (90 min)
12:15  Lunch
13:00  Meetings and collaboration
15:00  Shallow work batch: email, admin, follow-ups
16:30  Close the day (15 min)
```

This is an example, not a rule. The useful parts are the protected deep-work block, the batch for shallow work, and the close.

## Match the block to the task

| Task type | Block |
| --- | --- |
| Deep work | 60–90 minutes, no notifications |
| Meetings | The scheduled time plus ten minutes after |
| Shallow batch | One block for email, admin, and follow-ups |
| Waiting | A short weekly review, not a daily check |

Do not put deep work between two meetings. You will lose the first ten minutes to context switching.

## Leave buffers

A day with no gaps breaks on the first overrun. Leave fifteen minutes between blocks when you can. Keep one block free for the unexpected.

If every block is full, the plan is already late. Cut something.

## Use the Calendar view

In the personal vault, open the **Calendar** view to see the shape of the day or week. Scheduled tasks appear on their dates. If the day looks packed, move a task before it becomes overdue.

## Protect the block

- Close chat and email during deep work.
- Tell people when you are unavailable.
- Put the phone out of reach.
- If someone interrupts, write the request down and return to the block.

## Try it

Block tomorrow in your calendar:

- One 90-minute deep-work block for your must task.
- One 30-minute shallow-work batch.
- One 15-minute close.

Then check the Calendar view. If the blocks do not fit, move or cut a task now.
